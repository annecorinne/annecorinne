# Anne C. Carroll, PhD 👋

I’m a behavioral data scientist and marketing analytics lead with 14+ years of experience building measurement systems that help teams make better growth and retention decisions.

I specialize in turning multi-source marketing + customer data into **clear, defensible recommendations**—combining **SQL-first data work**, **rigorous experimentation**, and **practical modeling** (MMM/MTA) with stakeholder-ready storytelling.

---

## What I work on

- **Marketing measurement:** MMM, attribution (with incrementality reality checks), KPI design
- **Experimentation:** test design, power planning, causal inference, readouts teams can act on
- **Decision systems:** reusable datasets, metric layers, automated reporting, scenario planning
- **Analytics leadership:** cross-functional alignment, clarity on “what good looks like,” operating rhythms
